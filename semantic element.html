About MIT College

Our innovative teaching approach blends theoretical foundations with practical skills, preparing students for success in the competitive world of business and technology. At MIT, learning goes beyond the classroom. Through engaging case studies, hands-on computer training, group presentations, and term papers, students develop critical thinking and problem-solving abilities. We bring the industry to you with guest lectures from IT, banking, insurance, and corporate sectors. Our educational field visits provide a firsthand understanding of real-world applications. Join us at MIT to embark on a journey of academic excellence and holistic development. Become a part of a community that values knowledge, innovation, and practical experience. Shape your future with a degree from MIT and stand out in the global arena of business and technology.

BIT

Bachelor of Information Technology at MIT is a four-year, 129-credit undergraduate degree program based on a holistic understanding of information technology.

BBA

Bachelor of Business Administration at MIT is a three-year, 90-credit undergraduate degree program designed to provide students with a strong foundation in business principles and practices.

MBA

Master of Business Administration at MIT is a two-year, 60-credit graduate degree program that prepares students for leadership roles in the business world.
